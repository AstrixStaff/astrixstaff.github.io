Staff Oath

Zero Tolerance Policy

Violating any of the following policies will result in immediate disciplinary action, including demotion or removal from the Astrix Studio staff team.

Ownership of Contributions

All contributions made to Astrix Studio during your tenure remain the property of Astrix Studio.

Attempts to reclaim, dispute, or remove contributions after termination may result in permanent blacklisting.

Confidentiality

Leaking internal staff information, discussions, documentation, or decisions is strictly prohibited.

Consequences range from strikes to immediate removal depending on severity and management discretion.

Post Termination Conduct

Former staff members may not spread rumors, misrepresent internal matters, or leak sensitive information.

Violations may result in permanent blacklisting from Astrix Studio.

Resignation Terms

Resigned staff members must reapply for their position without special privileges.

Attempting to pressure or manipulate management for advantages after resignation will result in permanent blacklisting.

Professional Behaviour

Respect toward fellow staff and community members is mandatory.

Disrespect will result in a strike with no appeal.

Activity Requirements

Inactivity without submitting a formal inactivity request will result in a non appealable strike.

Role Model Expectations

All staff members are expected to represent Astrix Studio professionally in both public and internal environments.

Unprofessional or erratic behaviour may result in disciplinary action at management discretion.
