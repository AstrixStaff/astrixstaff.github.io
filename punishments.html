Minor Offenses

Incorrect Usage of Channels

This rule shouldn't be taken too seriously unless repeated frequently.

Offense #2: /warn <userid> Incorrect usage of channel
Offense #3: 5m – /mute <userid> 300s Incorrect usage of channel

Offense #2: /warn <userid> pinging staff

Offense #4: 3h – /mute <userid> 3h ping staff
