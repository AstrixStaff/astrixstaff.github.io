Mutes / Timeouts

Minor Offenses

Incorrect Usage of Channels

This rule shouldn't be taken too seriously unless repeated frequently.
Offense #1: Verbal Warning
Offense #2: !warn <userid> Incorrect usage of channel
Offense #3: 5m – !mute <userid> 300s Incorrect usage of channel
Offense #4: 3h – !mute <userid> 3h ping staff
Offense #5: 1d – !mute <userid> 1d ping staff
Note: Add 1h after exceeding offense #5.

Pinging Staff

If the user is pinging staff due to someone breaking a rule, exempt them.
Offense #1: Verbal Warning
Offense #2: !warn <userid> pinging staff
Offense #3: 30m – !mute <userid> 30m ping staff
Offense #4: 3h – !mute <userid> 3h ping staff
Offense #5: 1d – !mute <userid> 1d ping staff
Note: Add 1d after exceeding offense #5.

Advertising / Seeking Members

This is not allowed at astrix, especially advertising other commission servers.
Offense #1: !warn <userid> Advertising
Offense #2: 3h – !mute <userid> 3h Advertising
Offense #3: 6h – !mute <userid> 6h Advertising
Offense #4: 1d – !mute <userid> 1d Advertising
Offense #5: Perm – !mute <userid> Advertising

Spamming / Chat Flooding

Punish only if 4+ messages or long random messages without context.
Offense #1: 1h – !mute <userid> 1h Spamming
Offense #2: 3h – !mute <userid> 3h Spamming
Offense #3: 6h – !mute <userid> 6h Spamming
Offense #4: 12h – !mute <userid> 12h Spamming
Note: Double after #4 until 48h, then perm.

Toxicity / Staff Disrespect

Minor toxicity is allowed.
Offense #1: 3h – !mute <userid> 3h Toxicity
Offense #2: 6h – !mute <userid> 6h Toxicity
Offense #3: 12h – !mute <userid> 12h Toxicity
Offense #4: 24h – !mute <userid> 24h Toxicity
Note: After #4, double then perm.
