Staff Rules

Professional Conduct

All staff must communicate professionally with clients and internally. No favoritism, no public disputes, and no actions that harm the Astrix Studio brand.

Confidentiality

Client data, pricing structures, internal discussions, and freelancer performance records are strictly confidential.

Chain of Command

Report to HR first. Escalate to Staff Managers (SM) and then Managers if required. Do not bypass leadership without proper reason.

Brand Protection

Every decision reflects on Astrix Studio. Maintain discipline, clarity, and professionalism at all times.
