Staff Rules

All staff members below Manager must provide valid proof for every punishment issued.
Proof must be a full, uncropped screenshot unless management explicitly approves otherwise.
Failure to comply with any staff rule will result in disciplinary action.
All disciplinary violations operate on a two-strike system. Two strikes will result in removal. Severe breaches may escalate further and legal action may be taken where applicable.
Use common sense at all times. Do not engage in or create unnecessary drama.
Do not directly message higher staff regarding punishment decisions. Seek input from other staff members first and escalate through your Subdivision Lead or Management within proper channels.
Leaking any internal staff information, documentation, discussions, or decisions will result in immediate consequences.
Staffing on known competitors of SMP-FINDER is prohibited and may result in demotion.
Staff may not hold positions above Administrator on pay-to-win (P2W) servers.
Failure to maintain your mental health or properly communicate burnout may result in being placed on a forced Leave of Absence (LOA).
When blacklisting a server, valid proof must be shown to the affected user before escalation.
Asking for a promotion will automatically delay any future promotion consideration.
These rules are separate from the Staff Oath and exist to ensure structure, protection, and accountability for both staff and management.
