Staff Rules

All staff members below Manager must provide valid proof for every punishment issued.
Proof must be a full, uncropped screenshot unless management explicitly approves otherwise.
Failure to comply with any staff rule will result in disciplinary action.
All disciplinary violations operate on a three strike system. Three strikes will result in removal. Severe breaches may escalate further at management discretion.
Use common sense at all times. Do not create or engage in unnecessary drama.
Do not directly message higher staff regarding punishment decisions unless necessary. Seek input from other staff members first and escalate to Management through proper channels if required.
Leaking any internal staff information, documentation, discussions, or decisions will result in immediate disciplinary action.
Staffing on direct competitors of Astrix Studio may result in demotion or removal, depending on management discretion.
Staff may not hold positions above Administrator on pay to win servers while actively staffing for Astrix Studio.
Failure to maintain your mental health or communicate burnout may result in being placed on a forced Leave of Absence.
When blacklisting a server or individual, valid proof must be provided to the affected party before escalation.
Asking for a promotion will automatically delay any future promotion consideration.
These rules are separate from the Staff Oath and exist to ensure structure, accountability, and professionalism across Astrix Studio.
