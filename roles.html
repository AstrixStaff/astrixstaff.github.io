Staff Roles

Owner

Full authority over Astrix Studio.

Executive

Oversees management and direction.

Managers

Handle daily operations and supervise staff.

Staff Managers

Assist managers, supervise moderation, and oversee recruitment.

Human Resources

Handle internal issues, documentation, and recruitment processes.

Freelance Manager

Oversees freelance recruitment and manages freelancers.
